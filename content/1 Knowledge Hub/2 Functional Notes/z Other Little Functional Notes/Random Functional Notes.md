### Sunglasses
#### What it does??
1. **Block Harmful UV Rays**
    - Good sunglasses block **100% of UVA and UVB** rays from the sun, which helps prevent long-term eye damage like **cataracts**, **macular degeneration**, and **photokeratitis** (sunburn of the eye).
2. **Reduce Glare**:
    - Especially helpful while **driving**, **fishing**, **skiing**, or being near **water and snow**. Polarized lenses are designed specifically for this.
#### What to look while buying sun glasses?
 ###### UV Protection
- **Must-Have:** Look for labels that say "**100% UV protection**" or "**UV400**" (blocks all light rays with wavelengths up to 400 nm).
- Avoid buying just tinted lenses without UV protection—they can make your pupils dilate and cause **more harm** than not wearing glasses at all.

##### Lens Color
- **Grey/Smoke**: True color perception. Good general-purpose lens.
- **Brown/Amber**: Improves contrast, useful in hazy or variable light.
- **Yellow/Gold**: Enhances contrast in low light. Not ideal for bright sun.3
- **Green**: Reduces glare and brightens shadows. Balanced contrast and color.

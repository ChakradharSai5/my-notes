## Workout : 
I have made this plan keeping in mind this:
After discussing it, the conclusion was:
- While higher frequency can be beneficial, your priority is consistency.
- With your rotating shifts, a sustainable plan beats an "optimal" plan that you eventually stop following.
- Training each muscle every 5–8 days is still enough to maintain and gradually build muscle, especially as a natural lifter in a calorie deficit.
- Since fat loss is currently the primary objective, this frequency is acceptable.

April First Fortnight
​As of the latest available data for FY 2024-25, NTPC Mining dispatched 44.72 million metric tones (MMT) of coal to NTPC's power stations, marking a 26% year-on-year increase compared to the previous fiscal year .​
Coal production from NTPC’s five operational captive coal mines—Pakri Barwadih, Kerandari, and Chatti Bariatu in Jharkhand; Dulanga in Odisha; and Talaipalli in Chhattisgarh—reached 45.72 MMT during the same period . Additionally, the Amelia mine operated by THDC contributed to this output .​

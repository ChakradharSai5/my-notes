### Context

![[NGEF Module SLD Drawing.svg]]
[[NGEF Module SLD Drawing]]
- **NGEF (New Government Electrical Factory)** was a **government-owned electrical engineering company** based in **Bangalore, Karnataka, India**, established in **1956** with German collaboration.
- It produced **transformers, motors, switchgear, power electronics** and related industrial electrical equipment used in power plants, utilities, railways, etc.
- Government attempts to **privatize it failed**, and as it was considered **not viable**, the main NGEF entity was shut down in 2002 after losses.

# Khavda
Khavda is not a conventional single power plant. It is a gigantic renewable-energy complex in Kutch, Gujarat, combining solar + wind + battery storage + massive transmission infrastructure. The biggest developer there is Adani Green Energy, while NTPC Green Energy also has projects at Khavda.
- 📍 Location: Kutch, Gujarat.
- ⚡ Ultimate target: 30 GW of renewable generation — mainly solar + wind — across a huge area.
- 🏗️ Current status: Around 9–10 GW is already operational, with the rest being built toward the 2029 target.
- 🔋 Big new feature: About 3.37 GWh of battery storage has already been commissioned at Khavda.
- 🏭 Multiple developers: It's not one conventional power plant. Adani is the dominant developer, but NTPC Green and others also have projects there.
- 🔌 Why it's important: It is essentially a gigantic real-world test of solar + wind + battery + high-voltage transmission working together.

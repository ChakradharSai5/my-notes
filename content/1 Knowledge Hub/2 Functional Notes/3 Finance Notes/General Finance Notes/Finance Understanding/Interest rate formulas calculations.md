## 📌 Rule of 72 – Summary Table

| **Type**                    | **Formula**                                 | **Notes**                                                   |
|-----------------------------|----------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Exact Doubling Time**     | `t = ln(2) / ln(1 + r)`                      | `r` is annual interest rate as a **decimal** (e.g., 0.08)   |
| **Approximation (decimal)** | `t ≈ 0.693 / r`                              | Good for small `r` (e.g., 0.04 to 0.15)                     |
| **Rule of 72 (mental math)**| `t ≈ 72 / (r × 100)` or `t ≈ 0.72 / r`       | `r` in decimal (e.g., 0.08 for 8%)                          |
$$
FV = PV \times (1 + r)^t
$$
- The above one is the original formula from which this doubling thumb rule derived with $$FV=2 \times PV $$
- This we round to 72 thumb rule

### Bhubaneswar route from Kaniha
- The route we prefer to go by Kulei goes like Samal-Kulei-(almost Kamakhyanagar) Kamakhyanagar bypass -Dhenkennal - cuttak and enters by Vani vihar to Janpath roadthe other route is upto banarpal, from there to meramandali- Dhenkennal-Cuttack
- For both routes they merge at well before Dhenkennal, there is a square called Kamakhya square
- Also after connecting at Dhenkennal towards bhubaneswar, both travel same, and at near cuttak, there is two paths to go to, one is towards Cuttack, Phulnakara, Vanivihar
- Other divides to enter via Nandankan road from back side bhubaneswar at a Chowk called, "Bharat ratna Atal bihari vajpayee chowk", divides to Subash chandrabose setu via Nandankan zoo park to Patia Chandrasekahar pur to Nanadankan road to enter from back side of bhubaneswar

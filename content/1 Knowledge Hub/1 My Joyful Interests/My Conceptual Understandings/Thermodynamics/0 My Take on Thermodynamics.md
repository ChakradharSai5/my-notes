- Basically if somebody asks me about thermodynamics, especially about a fluid
- I'd say this:
See we can have two independent properties to define a fluid like mentioned in [[1 Thermodynamic State & Degrees of Freedom]], however, these independent properties shall be different with only liquid, only vapor vs liquid+Vapor state
during only L, only V, we can have P & T enough, but when a fluid is in liquid+Vapor state, we need one more x, or even better, P & S, or P & H, terms mentioned in [[1 Thermodynamic State & Degrees of Freedom]].

So in dealing with understanding of ACs or in thermal plant etc, we prefer to simply use P & H graphs , because the fluid state we want to change from complete L to complete V, then again to mixture etc.

And then we draw constant T lines, or constant S lines (if graph between P & H)

Where use these lines of constant T & S to understand, the behavior of different things, like for expansion valve, or compressor or Condenser or Turbine
For example you can see [[AC working mechanism]] for P-H diagram and we have dome of constant T line 
**And there is dome - which represents the fundamental physical property of a substance or fluid, like in it left most is liquid, right most is super saturated, and inside dome is vapor + liquid**
In [[AC working mechanism]], we take the fluid from 1-2-3-4 like this path of fluid and what's happening can be studied and we superimpose this with constant T lines or constant P lines.

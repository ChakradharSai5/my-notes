An **isentropic** process is an idealised process in which entropy remains constant.

This is very different from throttling.

### Ideal turbine

The ideal turbine expansion is approximately:

> **Isentropic**

The turbine takes the pressure/energy of the steam and converts part of it into shaft work.

### Throttle valve

The ideal throttling process is approximately:

> **Isenthalpic**

The pressure drops, but the valve does not extract useful shaft work.

Entropy increases.

This gives a very useful conceptual distinction:

|Device|Ideal thermodynamic model|
|---|---|
|Turbine|Isentropic expansion|
|Throttle valve|Isenthalpic throttling|

This distinction is extremely useful when analysing steam turbines.

This is the first application note that ties the conceptual notes together.

The refrigerant continuously cycles through four basic processes:

**Evaporator → Compressor → Condenser → Expansion Valve → Evaporator**

### Evaporator

Cold low-pressure refrigerant absorbs heat from room air.

Liquid evaporates into vapor.

The room loses heat.

### Compressor

Low-pressure vapor is compressed.

Pressure rises.

Temperature rises.

External work enters the refrigerant.

### Condenser

Hot high-pressure refrigerant rejects heat to outside air.

Vapor condenses into liquid.

### Expansion valve

High-pressure liquid is throttled to low pressure.

Enthalpy remains approximately constant.

The refrigerant moves toward the new low-pressure equilibrium state.

Some liquid may flash into vapor.

The resulting cold mixture enters the evaporator.

The cycle repeats.

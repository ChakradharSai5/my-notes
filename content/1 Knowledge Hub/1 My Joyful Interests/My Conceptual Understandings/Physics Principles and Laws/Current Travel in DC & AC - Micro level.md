### DC vs AC Electron motion
- In DC
	- the electron physically moves slowly along the wire, however not fast as we imagine, only mm/sec in **one direction**
	- But as soon as we switch on the supply the bulb glows, the reason being, a particular electron may reach the bulb slowly, but the domino effect like a water in pipe, if water pushed at one end, the other end water comes out right, similarly the effect of electron movement which is electric field shall move at fraction of speed of light speed, and so the bulb glows immediately
	- Hence current in constant
- In AC
	- the electron **do not** travel along the wire to the load or to the source
	- the electron moves back and forth may be a microns of distance, just back and forth 
	- Average displacement over a cycle in 0

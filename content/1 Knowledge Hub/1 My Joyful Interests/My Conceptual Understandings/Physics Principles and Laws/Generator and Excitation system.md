- ### Excitation system
- Basically you have to understand, we need to give rotor a DC current to rotor, only then 3 phase gets generated in stator
- but here how to give DC to rotor
- for that we have two ways 1. brushed
						2. brushless
#### Brush excitation system
- Here we simply take out put from the generator out put voltage, and stepdown by Excitation transformer, after stepping down, we convert this AC to DC, and that DC as rotor is rotating, DC is given through brushes
- And that makes the coil on the rotor behave like magnet and so this makes current in generator
#### Brushless excitation system
- Brushless mean, we want to skip using brushes, for that we made a jugaad
- for this, somehow we get DC to a static stator at excitor, and to complement this, on rotor we have coil rotated, this makes the DC current from stator of excitor shall make 3 phase on the rotor excitor as the rotor is in continuous rotation
- this generated AC on rotor excitor, shall be used to convert to DC using diode wheels - setup on rotor itself, shall make it DC, and this DC shall be given main rotor part coil. the same thing happens as it brush excitation system

#### Important Changes
- Gig and platform workers
	- Gig or platform workers, has to have social security of minimum 1-2% of annual company turnover, capped at 5% of payments
	- Aadhar linked UAN enables full portability
	- They are like freelancing, so here that 48Hrs rule dosen't applicable, but, social security applicable
		- Like PF, ESIC, insurance and other Security benifits
	- Like delivery boys, tech platforms, digital creators, contractors, freelancers
- FTEs
	-  Earlier FTEs-nothing but contract employees, were not given social security benefits like PF, leave, medical and social security coverage
	- Ensures equal wages and exercise use of contract labour
	- Earlier the gratuity is given if worked more than 1 year then you have to get gratuity
	- will receive a free annual health checkup
- Till now, companies can close a company if workers below 100 employees, without informing government, now it is capped to 300
- Women workers
	- Gender discrimination prohibited under law
	- Equal pay for equal work
	- Mandatory women's representation in GRC - Grievance comittee
		- atleast half of the employees
	- Family definition expanded to include parents in law 
		- Earlier only husband and son.
- MSME workers
	- all eligible MSME workers covered under social security code
	- Minimum wages, basic facilities
	- 48hours (not just MSME workers for all) per week, double the payment
- Salary should be paid before 7th of month
- Single registration, license and return, reducing compliance burden
- Pan India- ESIC coverage, including small and hazardous establishments
- Trade unions
	- 10 employees  or 10% of total employee whichever is lower can form an union
	- and management settles/discusses about disputes with the majority strength trade union only

##### Addiction

This isn't anyone's fault—it's just part of our unique traits and personalities. Some people fit easily into their surroundings, while others need a change of environment to find balance.

Many great individuals in history have channeled their need for connection into their passions and hobbies, turning their disconnect into world-changing achievements.

##### Self Respect
 - If you can’t trust yourself, then why should anyone else trust you?  If you want to build your confidence, begin by keeping promises to yourself.  When you consistently keep your promises to yourself, you establish a sense of trust and confidence in yourself.  You begin to see yourself as someone who is reliable and capable of following through on commitments, which can boost your self-esteem and self-worth.  When you follow through on those promises, you feel a sense of accomplishment and satisfaction. This feeling can fuel motivation for you to continue setting and achieving goals. Creating a positive cycle of growth and progress. It holds you accountable and helps you take ownership of your life and your choices. Making promises to yourself requires you to decide what is important to you and what you want to achieve. By keeping those promises, you are prioritizing your goals and values, which can help you stay focused and on track.  Keeping promises to yourself requires self-discipline, which is a valuable skill to cultivate. By developing self-discipline, you can improve your ability to resist temptation, delay gratification, and work towards long-term goals.  I’m not preaching grind mentality, no days off, etc. There are compromises within the promises you make to yourself.  Start with realistic promises, then build on them. Make a promise, execute, repeat.

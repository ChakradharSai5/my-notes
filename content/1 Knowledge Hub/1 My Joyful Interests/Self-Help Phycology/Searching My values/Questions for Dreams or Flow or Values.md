###### Questions 
Here are 3 questions my 45 year old self, would ask me today:  
1. What activity makes you completely lose track of time?  
When we get fully caught up into an activity, often times that means that it is worth exploring more. This does not count for scrolling ofcourse.   
2. Which object, person or experience brought you the most joy as a kid?  
Try to find out why this was. Did it gave you a sense of freedom? Ownership? Independence? What was it?  
3. If you live like the last 7 days forever, where do you end up? Would it lead you to a life well-lived or to a coffin full of regrets?  
It can be hard & challenging to think of this question, but it can be rewarding at the same time. Through reflection we grow. And all the wisdom in the world starts with self-awareness.

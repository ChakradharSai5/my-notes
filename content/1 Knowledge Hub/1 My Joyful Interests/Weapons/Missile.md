### Difference between Ballistic and Cruise Missile

| Feature         | **Ballistic Missile**                                                    | **Cruise Missile**                                                      |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Flight Path** | Arced, travels into space and re-enters atmosphere                       | Low-altitude, flat flight path like an aircraft                         |
| **Speed**       | Very fast: **supersonic to hypersonic**                                  | Mostly **subsonic**, some are **supersonic**                            |
| **Accuracy**    | High (modern), but traditionally lower                                   | Very high accuracy due to continuous guidance                           |
| **Range**       | Can be **short to intercontinental** (up to ~15,000 km)                  | Generally **short to medium range** (up to ~2,500 km)                   |
| **Payload**     | Nuclear or conventional warheads                                         | Usually conventional, but some can carry nuclear warheads               |
| **Examples**    | - **Agni-V** (India, ICBM) - **DF-41** (China) - **Minuteman III** (USA) | - **BrahMos** (India-Russia) - **Tomahawk** (USA) - **Nirbhay** (India) |

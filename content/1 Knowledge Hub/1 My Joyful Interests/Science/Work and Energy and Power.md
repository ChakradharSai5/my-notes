### History of Classical and Electrical Energy forms
Let’s build the full picture like a **story of discovery**, starting from the time people only knew about **mechanical energy** and had no clue about electricity.

---
## **1. Classical Mechanics & Energy (1600s–1700s)**
- Scientists like Galileo, Newton defined **force**, **mass**, **motion**.
- **Work** = Force × distance.
- **Energy** concept was born as “capacity to do work.”
- By mid-1800s, Joule showed **mechanical work ↔ heat** are equivalent
- Unit of energy = **Joule** → (1 N·m = 1 kg·m²/s²).  
    👉 At this stage, everything was about moving masses and heat. No electricity yet.
---
## **2. Electricity Appears (1700s)**

- People noticed rubbing amber attracts small objects (static electricity).
- Ben Franklin talked about “positive” and “negative” charges, but **no units yet**.
- “Current” was seen as some invisible fluid
---
## **3. Current & Charge (1800s)**
- Alessandro Volta built the **Voltaic pile** (battery) in 1800 → continuous electricity supply.
- Now people could study steady currents.
- **André-Marie Ampère (1820s):** discovered that two wires carrying current attract or repel.
    - Defined **current** as the cause of the magnetic effect.
    - Later, **1 Ampere** was defined as the current that, when flowing in two parallel wires 1 m apart, produces a certain force per meter of length.  
        👉 This gave a _mechanical definition of current_.
- From current, they defined **charge**:
    Q=I⋅tQ=I⋅t
    so **1 Coulomb = charge transported by 1 Ampere in 1 second.**  
    (At this stage, they still didn’t know charges were electrons!)
---
## **4. Voltage (1800s)**
- Michael Faraday studied how current does work (electrolysis).
- They realized “there’s a potential difference that pushes charges.”
- Defined **voltage (Volt)** as:
    1 V=1 J/1 C1V=1J/1C
    meaning: 1 Joule of energy is given to 1 Coulomb of charge when moved across 1 Volt.  
    👉 Tied electricity directly back to the Joule.
---
## **5. Resistance & Ohm (1827)**
- Georg Ohm found:
    V=I⋅RV=I⋅R
- Defined **Ohm (Ω)** as the resistance when 1 Volt drives 1 Ampere.  
    👉 Now the “electrical triangle” was complete: Voltage, Current, Resistance.
---
## **6. Electrons Discovered (1897)**
- J. J. Thomson discovered the **electron** as a particle carrying negative charge.
- Measured the electron’s charge-to-mass ratio.
- Later, Millikan’s oil drop experiment (1909) gave the **charge of one electron**:
    e≈1.6×10−19 Ce≈1.6×10−19C
👉 Now, Coulomb was connected to **real particles**:
1 C=6.24×1018 electrons1C=6.24×1018electrons

---
## **7. Modern SI Refinement (20th–21st century)**
- At first, Ampere was defined via force between wires.
- Today (since 2019 SI redefinition), we **fix the value of the elementary charge** ee.
- So:
    - **1 Coulomb = exactly 1/(1.602176634×10−19)1/(1.602176634×10−19) electrons**.
    - **1 Ampere = exactly 1/(1.602176634×10−19)1/(1.602176634×10−19) electrons per second.**
👉 Everything is now locked back to fundamental constants.
## **Summary of the Chain**
1. **Energy (Joule)** came first — from mechanics.
2. **Current (Ampere)** defined via magnetic force.
3. **Charge (Coulomb)** defined as current × time.
4. **Voltage (Volt)** defined as energy per charge (Joule per Coulomb).
5. **Resistance (Ohm)** tied the three together.
6. **Electron** discovered → charge became particle-based.
    
7. **Modern SI**: now everything ties back to fundamental constants (electron charge, Planck’s constant, etc.).

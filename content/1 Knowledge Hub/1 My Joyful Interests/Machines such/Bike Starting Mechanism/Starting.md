Typical starter motors in bikes:
The stator can either be:
Permanent magnets (common in small bikes and scooters)
OR
Electromagnetic windings (common in bigger bikes or cars)
In many bikes, yes — the starter motor stator is a permanent magnet type.
That simplifies the design and reduces size/weight.
✅ When you press the start button:
Battery sends DC current.
Current flows through the brushes onto the commutator, and then into the rotor windings (armature).
The rotor (armature) now carries a current.
Now, by Lorentz Force Law,
A current-carrying conductor (the rotor coil) inside a magnetic field (from the permanent magnet stator) experiences a force.
This force acts perpendicular to both the magnetic field and current direction → produces torque → causes rotation of the rotor shaft.
This is the basic principle of DC motor operation.

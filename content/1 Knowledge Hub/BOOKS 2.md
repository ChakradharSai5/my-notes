### 📖 How I use this list

**This is a menu, not a backlog.**

⭐ **NOW** → I'm already interested; continue while the interest is alive.  
🟢 **A** → First place to look when I'm in that category's mood.  
🟢 **B** → Excellent alternatives after A, or when their description attracts me more.  
🟡 **MAYBE** → Ignore unless that specific book/topic suddenly attracts me.

**I don't have to finish a book because I started it. I don't have to read a book because I own it. And starting a series does not commit me to the series.**

**Choose by current curiosity, then read—not by endlessly researching what to read.**

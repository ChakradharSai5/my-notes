What a Grid Coupling is good at
Grid Coupling
A grid coupling uses a spring-like steel grid between two hubs

Advantages:
Excellent shock absorption
Dampens vibration
Handles motor starting shocks well
Good for motor-to-gearbox connections
Relatively easy maintenance

Typical location:
Motor → Grid Coupling → Gearbox

Reason:
The motor side experiences:

Start-stop shocks
Torsional vibrations
Transient loads

The flexible grid acts like a spring and protects both the motor and gearbox.


What a Gear Coupling is good at

Gear Coupling

A gear coupling consists of external and internal gear teeth that mesh together.

Advantages:

Very high torque capacity for its size
Can accommodate some misalignment
Extremely robust for heavy-duty applications
Suitable for slow-speed, high-torque shafts

Typical location:
Gearbox Output → Driven Equipment

Reason:
After the reduction gearbox:

Speed is low
Torque is very high
Shock loads from the driven machine may be significant

Gear couplings are mechanically strong and can transmit enormous torque without requiring a very large coupling size. ^YsKoZuKm

     supply to that relay, the main contact in that shall make to close (without supply it was Open contact)

via control transformer, typically, the 3 phase supply from every switchgear tapped as single phase and that 
shall be given to typically two transformers called control transformer, from which 110V control supply
is taken, and used. This ensures that control supply can only go away only if the 3 phase
to the switchgear is gone, in which case drive shall not have 3ph, and so no bother about
